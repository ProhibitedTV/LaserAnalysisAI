# Understanding Results

LaserLab uses image processing to detect and quantify structure. A detection is **not** by itself proof of a message, a language, or an unusual origin.

## Why review is blinded

During **Review**, source labels are concealed so the reviewer can inspect candidate patterns without knowing whether an image came from the laser or control footage. Notes are associated with blind IDs. After review is marked complete, the explicit **Unblind and compare** step reveals attribution and computes matched-control comparisons.

This sequence helps reduce confirmation bias. It does not remove all experimental confounds.

## Reading the evidence ladder

| Level | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **No signal** | The measured laser features did not exceed the chosen control baseline. |
| **Artifact** | Control behavior or processing better explains the apparent detection. |
| **Anomaly** | Something scored unusually, but the controlled evidence is insufficient. |
| **Above-control candidate** | Predefined score and statistical thresholds were exceeded compared with controls. |
| **Repeatable candidate** | Above-control evidence also shows persistence or repeatability under the configured method. |

Even a repeatable candidate is only evidence about measured image features under this protocol. It is not independent proof of language, communication, consciousness, or origin. Additional independent experiments would be needed.

## Why OCR sometimes prints text

Optical character recognition can generate plausible letters from noise, fringes, and textures. Examine the crop and preprocessing method, compare with null controls, and repeat with newly recorded data. A readable string is not automatically meaningful.

## What a null result means

The current recordings, detector settings, and control baseline did not establish a difference. Null findings are useful; they do not disprove every possible explanation of a subjective observation.

## Sharing results for review

Include protocol, version, capture geometry, control design, exclusions, and privacy-reviewed output. Avoid selective screenshots of only the most striking crops. Keep individual observations separate from image-detector scores. See [Community and Privacy](Community-and-Privacy.md).
