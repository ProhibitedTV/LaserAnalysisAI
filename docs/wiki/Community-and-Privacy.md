# Community, Contributions, and Privacy

This is a home for optical experiments, reproducible analysis, carefully documented observations, honest criticism, and useful null results. We welcome beginners and experienced engineers alike.

## Where to go

- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ProhibitedTV/LaserAnalysisAI/discussions)** for interpretation questions, controlled experiment ideas, and community conversation.
- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ProhibitedTV/LaserAnalysisAI/issues/new/choose)** for reproducible defects, feature requests, or documented scientific validity concerns.
- **[Contribution guide](https://github.com/ProhibitedTV/LaserAnalysisAI/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)** for code, tests, documentation, and review standards.

Do not use Issues for claims of proof without a reproducible protocol, raw data authorization, and meaningful controls. Be specific and charitable when challenging an interpretation.

## Before sharing

1. Keep footage local by default. The app does not automatically upload it.
2. Obtain consent for any recognizable people or participant reports.
3. Remove names, account IDs, geolocation, private paths, and unrelated personal material.
4. Check media licenses and attribution before redistribution.
5. Prefer privacy-reviewed, blinded review bundles; unblinded exports can expose source roles and metadata.
6. Never post secrets, exploits, or sensitive vulnerability details publicly.

Subjective experience notes must remain separately labeled context, not image-scoring evidence. This community does not provide substance-use instructions or unsafe experimental directions.

## Respectful inquiry

Curiosity is welcome; unwarranted certainty, hostility, personal attacks, and stigmatizing participants are not. Explain what your experiment did test and what it did not. A null result still counts as progress.
