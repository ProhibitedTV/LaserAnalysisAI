# LaserLab Community Code of Conduct

LaserLab exists to make difficult questions easier to test. Curiosity, skepticism, learning, and respectful disagreement all belong here.

## Our commitments

- Welcome people regardless of background or experience. Explain terms and help beginners find the right resources.
- Debate methods and evidence rather than attacking people. Different interpretations are welcome when honestly distinguished from measurements.
- Avoid harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and disruptive personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, licensing, consent, and laser safety. Do not post confidential or identifying research materials without permission.
- Do not use this community to promote unsafe experimentation, substance procurement, or unsupported scientific certainty.
- Describe null results and failed replications accurately; they help improve the methodology.

## Reporting and moderation

For ordinary community concerns, contact project maintainers through a non-public channel when one is available. Avoid posting a victim's personal details publicly. Moderators may hide, edit, or remove violating content, restrict participation, or close conversations to protect the community. For potentially exploitable security bugs, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public report.

When no private moderation contact is configured, request one without sharing sensitive details. Project maintainers should publish a private contact route as the community grows.
